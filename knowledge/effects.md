# Glitch! Effects

## Glitch
- **Access:** Free
- **Works on video:** Yes
- **How to use:** Touch the screen. Swipe from the bottom left (no errors) to the top right (maximum errors).

## PNG
- **Access:** Premium
- **Works on video:** Yes
- **How to use:** Touch the screen.
- **Description:** A real PNG format glitch with five filtering algorithm types: None, Sub, Up, Average and Paeth.

## WEBP
- **Access:** Free
- **Works on video:** Yes
- **How to use:** A real WEBP format glitch. touch the screen anywhere to apply the effect. the glitch always appear vertically from bottom to top.

## Paintbrush
- **Access:** Free
- **Works on video:** Yes
- **How to use:** Touch the screen, keep pressed and move your finger to add glitches.

## Field
- **Access:** Premium
- **Works on video:** Yes
- **How to use:** Play with the field frequencies.
- **Description:** Alters images with field asymptotes, simulating cathode ray tube distortion.

## Hacker
- **Access:** Free
- **Works on video:** No
- **How to use:** Hack the screen by scrambling pixels by touching it several times.

## Wave
- **Access:** Free
- **Works on video:** No
- **How to use:** Swipe left, right, up and down to apply effect.

## Triangle
- **Access:** Free
- **Works on video:** No

## Datamosh
- **Access:** Premium
- **Works on video:** Video only
- **How to use:** Press the video icon and pick one or two videos, then adjust Range, Intensity and Displace.
- **Description:** Manipulates your videos with the famous datamosh effect. With two videos, the second melts into the last frame of the first. The result is saved as MP4.

## Animation
- **Access:** Premium
- **Works on video:** No, it creates animations from your pictures
- **How to use:** Touch the screen to record frames, then save and select the frames to keep.
- **Description:** Transforms static images into animations, saved as MP4, GIF or both.

## Video
- **Access:** Premium
- **Works on video:** Video only
- **How to use:** Press the video icon to load or record a video, select the frames, then choose an effect on the first frame.
- **Description:** Applies the chosen effect to each selected frame. Only the first 5 seconds of the video are used. The result is saved as MP4, GIF or both.
