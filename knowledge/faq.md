# Glitch! FAQ

## Exporting

### How do I export a video in HD?
Videos have no separate HD setting, because the Resolution option only applies to photos. The quality depends on the tool you use:

- **Video effect:** the MP4 keeps the resolution of your original video, so start from an HD video and save as MP4.
- **Datamosh:** the video is saved as MP4, with its longest side limited to 1280 pixels.
- **GIF:** GIFs are scaled to 480 pixels high, so choose MP4 when quality matters.

The Video effect and Datamosh are premium features.

### How do I change the resolution of my photos?
Open **Resolution** from the app menu and choose LOW, MID, HIGH or FULL. LOW (up to about 1 megapixel) is available to everyone. MID (about 5 megapixels), HIGH (about 8 megapixels) and FULL require premium. High resolutions can occasionally make the saved picture look different from the preview.

### Where are my photos and videos saved?
Photos are saved in `Pictures/ImageGlitcher` and videos and animations in `Movies/ImageGlitcher` on your device. You can also find them in your gallery app.

### Which file formats can I save?
Photos are saved as JPG. Animations and videos can be saved as MP4, GIF or both, and Datamosh videos are saved as MP4. The PNG and WEBP effects recreate the look of those formats' corruption, but the resulting photo is still saved as JPG.

## Premium

### What does the premium version include?
Premium unlocks all premium effects and the higher photo resolutions (MID, HIGH and FULL). The premium effects are PNG, Field, Delaunay, Pixel Sort, Warp, Ghost, Zalgo, Chromatic, Datamosh, Animation and Video.

### What subscription plans are available?
Premium is available as a monthly or a yearly subscription. Both unlock the same features. The current price for your country is shown on the Premium screen, along with any free trial or yearly savings on offer.

### How do I restore my purchases?
Open **Premium** from the app menu and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you are using the same Google account you used for the purchase.

### I bought premium or a single effect in the past. Do I still have it?
Yes. Earlier one-time premium purchases and single-effect purchases stay valid, so you do not need a subscription to keep them. If they are not showing, use **Restore Purchases** on the Premium screen.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Google Play. In the Play Store app, open your profile, go to **Payments & subscriptions**, then **Subscriptions**, select Glitch! and cancel. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

## Effects

### How do I use Datamosh?
1. Choose **Datamosh** in the left panel (premium required).
2. Press the video icon and pick one video, or two. With two videos, the second melts into the last frame of the first.
3. Use **Range** to select the portion of the video to affect.
4. Set **Intensity**. Higher values create more dramatic results.
5. Optionally turn on **Displace** to change the type of effect.
6. Save the result. The MP4 appears in `Movies/ImageGlitcher`.

### How do I apply a glitch to a photo?
Load a picture with the top buttons, then choose an effect in the left panel. Touch and swipe the image: with the Glitch effect, the bottom left corner means no errors and the top right corner means maximum errors. Other effects respond to touch differently, and the app shows a short hint for each one. Use the top buttons to save or share the result.

### How do I create an animation?
Choose **Animation** in the left panel (premium required) and touch the screen to record frames as you glitch. You can load more pictures with the camera icon. Press save to confirm, select the frames to keep, then set the speed (5 to 60 FPS, 25 by default), optionally reverse the animation, and choose MP4, GIF or both. Pressing the back button clears all the animation frames.

### Which effects can I use on a video?
With the **Video** tool (premium required), you can use Glitch, PNG, WEBP, Paintbrush, Field, Pixel, Deep-Fried, XOR, Scanner, Pixel Sort, Quake, Ghost, VHS, 3D, Zalgo and Chromatic. Press the video icon to load or record a video, select the frames, and choose the effect on the first frame. Only the first 5 seconds of the video are used.

## Troubleshooting

### Why won't my photo or video load?
Files from Google Photos or other cloud apps can be blocked by access restrictions. Save the file to your device first, then pick it from the device's storage. If you see "Couldn't load the video", pick the video again. If the app reports that access was not granted, allow the requested permission and try again.

### How do I contact support?
Email glitch4ndroid@gmail.com and describe the problem, including your device model and the app version if you can. If the app shows its error screen, you can also use its **Send E-Mail** button.
